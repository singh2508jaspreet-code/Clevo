About Cleevo

The Problem: Traditional cleaning products rely on harsh chemicals that harm health and the environment.

Our Solution: Cleevo offers plant-powered, powder-to-liquid cleaners that are safe, effective, and sustainable.

Our Mission: To make natural cleaning accessible, affordable, and powerful for every household.

Our Vision: A future where clean homes and a clean planet coexist.

Cleevo exists to create a healthier home and a cleaner planet. We believe effective cleaning should never come at the cost of human health or environmental damage. Our mission is to make natural cleaning accessible, affordable, and powerful.
